# radiant — Map

> *The Prime Radiant showed the Plan's equations in the air, and the Speakers wrote
> their corrections beside them, each in their own color.* — after Asimov, Second
> Foundation, 1953

The Mentat thinks beside the sovereign and changes minds, not files. Its book carries
the office across windows; what the book cannot carry is a map — a sitting's whole
exploration, wide, drawn, with the evidence beside each claim. Until 2026-09-04 that
map died in the conversation, the office's founding sin, or was flattened into a book
entry a dozen lines long. The neck sitting (mentat-05, 2026-09-03 → 04) drew a map too
wide for the book, Felix read it five times in a day, and ruled: *"It demands, nay, it
inspires a new artifact, not a mere book entry … I want a visual journey."* He named
it in the room: **Radiant**. He also lost an hour of his own writing to a stray
keystroke the same night — the expression bottleneck biting the sitting about
bottlenecks. The first Radiant carries that as its founding scar.

## 1. The bet

A sitting's thinking survives as a designed map that two readers can use cold: Felix,
at human speed, re-reading until it ignites; and a future Mentat, booting from the
index and the last head in two minutes. Written down, the conditions that made a
sitting ignite become a recipe that can be run again — and tested, because a recipe
that produces the feeling without the change was a costume.

Felix's two wants for it — *one page, maximally compressed* and *a hundred pages, every
obscure reference* — are one artifact with three depths (§2), not a compromise
between them.

## 2. Anatomy of a Radiant

| Part | Reads in | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| **Head** | two minutes | the question in the summoner's words; the computation in at most seven lines; the levers; the recipe; reading times |
| **Body** | an hour | the journey in the order the sitting ran it — chapters, with figures that show a mechanism a cold reader would otherwise assemble from prose |
| **Datums** | inside the body | numbers about Felix from his own files, each with the script that produced it and its caveats |
| **Guard** | five minutes | every slap of the sitting in one place — the pushbacks, proportional to the praise |
| **Rabbit holes** | a lifetime | the annotated bibliography: name · year · one line · the spoke it feeds |
| **Colophon** | one minute | who, when, at what tier; the capture ledger — what went where |

## 3. How a Radiant is made

1. **In the sitting.** The Mentat drafts the page as the sitting runs; Felix redlines;
   the page iterates until he calls it. The sitting's own tiny changes are made by
   tiny changes.
2. **At the close.** The Radiant seals (hard law 2). The book gets its entry, pointing
   here; `LEDGER.md` gets its line; the index below gets its row.
3. **After.** Codas append. A correction Felix makes later is a dated coda, never a
   silent edit — the record of what the sitting thought is the point.

## 4. The recipe — a sitting that ignites (from the neck sitting's own analysis)

Six conditions, all present on 2026-09-03; the horizon sitting (mentat-03) shared the
topic and a datum but not the form, and is the control:

- A **wide question about a load-bearing meta-thing** — the process, with Felix as a
  component — rather than a building. Ration these: they are also the process-front
  trap.
- **The long form licensed** in Felix's own words. The waggle taste is the default
  and binds until he opens the valve.
- **Breadth first**, depth on his picks. Seventy branches are seventy chances for a
  phrase to catch.
- **Prior art imported and defined inline**, from many disciplines — the
  expand-awareness stance at full throttle, not as garnish.
- **One datum about Felix from the record** — measured, never praised.
- **A guard proportional to the praise**, the office's own test applied to itself.

And **a night between turns**: the two sittings Felix has praised hardest both spanned
days. Two of two — a small n, and the mechanism the sitting itself named
(incubation).

**The anti-recipe.** A recipe followed produces the semblance of a sitting. Judge a
sitting by its contract, not its costume: what changed in Felix's decisions and files in
the week after. A sitting that ran the six lines and changed nothing was a performance,
however it felt.

## 5. Non-goals (defended)

- **Not a board.** Radiants are drawn in sittings, never laid as charges; there is no
  batch, no tender, no `Done when:` on a Radiant.
- **Not law.** Nothing here binds anything. Canon-shaped conclusions travel as inbox
  entries to the Grand Architect's desk; building-shaped conclusions to that
  building's inbox.
- **Not a product.** No app, no server, no build step, no framework. A page and its
  data.
- **Not the book.** `SAPHO.md` stays the office's diary; a Radiant never replaces an
  entry, and a pulse sitting gets a book line only. A Radiant is drawn when the
  sitting earned a map.
- **Not a rendering target yet.** Stigmergon may someday show Radiants in the room;
  that is a stigmergon chapter, laid on its board, not this building's work.

## 6. The board

None. If tooling is ever wanted — a renderer, a lint for the anatomy, an index
generator — instantiate `board.md` from the templates then, and not before.

## 7. Working agreements (project physics)

- One directory per Radiant: `NNN-<name>/index.html` + `data/` (scripts and their
  outputs). Ids are monotonic, never reused; the name is Felix's and may change — the
  id does not.
- Felikai is inlined verbatim, header comment included; drift from hexwright's canon
  is noted in the Radiant's colophon when it happens.
- Published privately via the Artifact tool as the glass over the file. The URL rides
  the colophon and the ledger line.
- Nothing about a third party that they did not say in the record; Felix's own words
  are quoted, never paraphrased into stronger claims.
