session's at his word. Entry format: `- ⟨YYYY-MM-DD⟩ · ⟨who⟩ · ⟨what⟩` — one
bullet per entry; an entry needing evidence becomes a `---`-separated block opening
with that line. The Mentat sweeps at the next sitting here: each entry is ruled —
distilled into MAP.md, appended to a Radiant as a coda, or rejected — then deleted;
the ledger line records rejections, and git keeps the bytes (entries are committed
before they are cleared). A swept inbox is empty.
